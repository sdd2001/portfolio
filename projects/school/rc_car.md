School project with a friend, turning a remote-controlled F1 car into a first-person-view vehicle.
We fitted an onboard computer and camera, programmed it to receive wireless steering inputs, and built a simple interface with gamepad support.
The result: a live camera feed you can drive through with a standard game controller.
